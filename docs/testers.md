Hi! Thanks for trying Statement Swipe, an app I'm building for checking your credit card statement one
purchase at a time. Swipe right on purchases you recognize, left to look closer at one you don't, and up
to file one away (to split with friends, get reimbursed, or keep for taxes).

Your statement never leaves your phone: the app reads it right on the device, with no accounts and no
tracking.

1. Open **statement-swipe.vercel.app** on your phone.
2. **Add it to your Home Screen before importing anything.** On iPhone, in Safari: tap ⋯ beside the
   address bar, then Share, View More, Add to Home Screen, and Add. On Android, tap Install when the app
   offers it. Statements added in the browser don't move over to the Home Screen app.
3. Open it from your Home Screen and take the short tour.
4. Download your latest credit card statement as a PDF from your bank's app, and import it. "How do I get
   my file?" on the import screen has steps for most banks.
5. Review it, file a few purchases, and check the Tasks tab.

What I'd love to hear: anything confusing, broken, slow, or missing, and whether you'd use it every
month. Text me, or use Send feedback in Settings (the gear).

If the app says your statement's purchases don't add up, or finds none, please tap "Help fix this for
your bank" and send me what it shows. Every letter and number is masked, so there's nothing personal in
it, and it teaches the app to read your bank's statements.
